FlockIQ

Flock monitoringDatabase-driven

FlockIQ is a flock monitoring system for a poultry farm, built around a batch of chicks from the day they arrive until harvest. It tracks the birds, the feed, the vaccines, the money spent, and the profit or loss. It is meant to replace paper records and show clearly where the money goes and which batches earn.

🔔Health alertson

Worker records

Submits daily deaths and the feed given, which is deducted from inventory automatically. Once a week, submits a sample weighing to get the average bird weight.

Manager verifies and runs

Approves or rejects the Worker’s records with a reason. Tracks expenses, schedules the Day 12 and Day 16 vaccines, keeps the vitamin, medicine and feed inventories, and harvests the batch.

Owner decides

Sets up each incoming batch, sets the market price used to judge profit, manages user accounts, and reads the results in Summary Reports and Harvest History.

How the numbers connect

  • Remaining birds: initial count minus deaths and culls.
  • Profit or loss: harvest value at the market price minus total expenses. A gain shows green and a loss shows red.
  • Performance: ADG shows how fast the birds gained weight. FCR shows how much feed it took per kilogram gained.
  • Best Season of Sales: compares Dry and Wet season profits over the year, so the Owner can see which season earns more.
